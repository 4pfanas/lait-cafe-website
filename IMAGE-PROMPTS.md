# Lait: photo slots

**Status:** all slots are filled with Lait's own Talabat and Instagram photos, plus one generated image (`room-cushion`). The signature cards now show Velvet Cloud and Ocean Latte, and the hero square shows the Hot Matcha Latte. The prompts below are kept for any future swaps.

Every photo on the site is optional. Until a file exists, the slot shows a paper panel with a hand-drawn doodle, so the site never looks broken.

To add a photo, save it in `src/assets/images/` using the slot name below (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif`) and rebuild. Astro resizes and compresses it automatically.

**Best option:** real photos from Lait's own Instagram (@lait.ae), with their permission. The AI prompts below are a fallback for the pitch only. Swap them for real photos before going live.

**House style for every shot:** bright, airy, white and milk tones. Soft daylight from a big window. Warm cream and light-wood surfaces. One cognac or caramel accent at most. No neon, no dark moody lighting, no text in the image.
